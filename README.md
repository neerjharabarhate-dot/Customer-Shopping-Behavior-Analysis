The company wants to identify how customer demographics, product preferences, discounts, shipping methods, customer reviews, and subscription status relate to purchasing behavior and revenue.

How can the company use customer shopping data to identify purchasing trends, improve customer engagement, and optimize marketing and product strategies?

## 🔄 5. Project Workflow

The project follows a structured end-to-end data analytics workflow.

### 01. Business Problem Understanding

Defined the business objectives, identified key analytical questions, and established the scope of the project.

### 02. Python – Data Cleaning & Preparation

Used Python to clean, transform, and prepare the raw dataset for analysis.

**Key Activities:**

1. **Missing Value Treatment:** Imputed 37 missing review ratings using the median rating of each product category.
2. **Column Standardization:** Converted column names into lowercase snake_case format.
3. **Feature Engineering:** Created an `age_group` column to categorize customers into four age groups.
4. **Data Transformation:** Converted purchase frequency categories into numeric day values.
5. **Redundant Column Removal:** Removed `promo_code_used` after confirming it matched `discount_applied` across all records.
6. **Data Validation:** Verified that no missing values remained after the cleaning process.

### 03. SQL – Business Analysis & Insights

Used SQL to analyze customer shopping behavior and answer key business questions.

**Key Analysis Performed:**

1. **Revenue Analysis:** Compared total revenue generated by male and female customers.
2. **Discount & Spending Analysis:** Identified customers using discounts whose purchase amounts were at or above the overall average.
3. **Product Rating Analysis:** Ranked the top five products by average review rating.
4. **Shipping Analysis:** Compared average purchase amounts across Standard and Express shipping.
5. **Subscription Analysis:** Compared customer counts, average spending, and revenue between subscribers and non-subscribers.
6. **Discount Dependency Analysis:** Identified the top five products by percentage of purchases with discounts applied.
7. **Customer Segmentation:** Classified customers into New, Returning, and Loyal segments based on previous purchases.
8. **Product Popularity Analysis:** Identified the top three most purchased products within each category.
9. **Repeat Buyer Analysis:** Compared subscription status among customers with more than five previous purchases.
10. **Age Group Revenue Analysis:** Compared total revenue across different customer age groups.

### 04. Power BI – Interactive Dashboard & Visualization

Developed an interactive Power BI dashboard to present key performance indicators and customer shopping trends.

**Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)**

- Total Customers
- Total Purchase Revenue
- Average Purchase Amount
- Average Review Rating

**Dashboard Visualizations**

- Customer Distribution by Subscription Status
- Revenue by Gender
- Revenue by Product Category

**Interactive Slicers**

- Gender

- Shipping Type
- Payment Method

### 05. Insights & Business Recommendations

Analyzed the results to identify opportunities for improving customer engagement, product performance, and revenue generation.
